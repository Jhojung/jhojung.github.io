Research

May 2026 – Present

Feasibility Study of Cryogenic Computing in Space-Based Data Centers

Sungkyunkwan Univ.

Industry-Academia Collaboration Project
(Advised by Prof. Dongmoon Min)

Investigating whether the space environment can eliminate the cooling overhead that has historically limited terrestrial cryogenic computing, by modeling thermal characteristics, auxiliary cooling subsystems, and total cost of ownership (TCO) of orbital data centers.

Building analytical and simulation-based models to estimate TCO-per-performance for both standard and cryogenically operated space data centers, with the goal of characterizing the design space and identifying conditions under which cryogenic orbital deployment is or is not justified.
